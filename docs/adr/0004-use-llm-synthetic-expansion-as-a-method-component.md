# Use contract-first LLM synthetic expansion as a method component

The training paper will treat LLM synthetic expansion as a first-class method component rather than incidental data generation, but synthetic data will be contract-first rather than free-form. A structured behavioral contract must precede generated DUTs, testbenches, checkers, variants, or faults; LLMs propose candidates, while provenance checks, contamination checks, independent contract checks, EVAS validation, Spectre shadow audit, and diversity controls decide whether examples can support training claims.
